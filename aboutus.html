Our story

Born from a single plastic cup.

EcoBoba Co. started in 2021 when two friends counted the cups piling up after one weekend market. They decided bubble tea could taste incredible without leaving a mess behind, so they built a menu around compostable cups, plant-based pearls and tea from fair-trade farms.

Our mission

Make every sip regenerative: zero plastic, minimal waste, and fair pay across our supply chain, from leaf to cup.

Our vision

A world where the default way to enjoy a drink on the go leaves the planet better off than before.

What we value

Planet first

Compostable packaging, solar-powered kitchens and a refill discount on every reusable cup.

Fair partnerships

We buy direct from small tea growers and pay above fair-trade minimums.

Honest flavour

Real ingredients, less sugar, and pearls made from plants. No shortcuts.
